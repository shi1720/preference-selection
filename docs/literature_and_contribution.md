# Literature positioning and novelty audit

Search date: 16 October 2026. This is a targeted research review, not a registered
systematic review or proof that no overlapping study exists.

The initial topics considered were AI-assisted customs qualification, learning
to use agreements, supplier substitution, and agreement-use measurement. The
first three have substantial existing research or commercial implementations.
The selected contribution is the quantitative Swiss reversal and a reproducible
mixed-chapter selection interval, with clearly stated limitations.

## Main searches

- Preference utilization, compliance costs, learning, and rules of origin.
- UK 2021–2024 preference utilization and the DCTS reform.
- Swiss 2024 industrial tariff abolition and preference-use changes.
- Preference utilization with composition, denominator changes, decomposition,
  fixed weights, index numbers, and Simpson reversals.
- Swiss official FTA monitoring and industrial-tariff policy documentation.
- Public and commercial free-trade-agreement qualification systems.

The search did not establish a prior paper reporting this exact UK–Swiss
2023–2024 decomposition and mixed-chapter interval. Absence from the searched
results does not establish global novelty. The core ratio-selection logic and
standardization are established mathematics. The interval is a specialized
accounting result whose contribution depends on its empirical use and transparent
treatment of the agricultural exceptions.

## Adversarial assessment

The strongest objection is that the official statistical definition already
anticipates this issue, and a research audience may regard the empirical result
as a useful but incremental measurement note. The draft acknowledges that fact.
Its substantive strengths are transparent real data, exact reproduction, an
important policy event, a negative weighted surviving-sector result, explicit
mixed-product uncertainty, and sensitivity results that are not selectively hidden.

The current evidence does not justify describing the paper as a groundbreaking
behavioral discovery, an estimate of welfare gains, an experimentally validated
compliance product, or a high-probability acceptance at a selective trade seminar.
Tariff-line legal mapping and linked firm records would materially strengthen
future work, but are not claimed to have been acquired or analyzed here.
