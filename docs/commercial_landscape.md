# Commercial application and existing alternatives

Desk review, 16 October 2026. No customers were interviewed; no market-size or
willingness-to-pay estimates are inferred from trade values.

| Existing offering | Observed public capability | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| [DBT preference-use statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/preference-utilisation-of-uk-trade-in-goods) | Public eligible/use and tariff-free tables | Basic reporting is already publicly available |
| [SECO/EFTA agreement-use dashboard](https://www.seco.admin.ch/en/use-of-free-trade-agreements) | Utilization and customs-savings measures | Savings-oriented monitoring is an established practice |
| [Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE FTA Management](https://africa.thomsonreuters.com/en/products-services/global-trade-management/free-trade-agreement.html) | Qualification, rules-of-origin and compliance workflows | A generic automated-origin product is not a new category |
| [Descartes Free Trade Intelligence](https://www.descartes.com/resources/news/descartes-introduces-new-ai-powered-free-trade-intelligence-fti-solution-help-reduce?language=en) | Publicly described origin analysis, tariff-shift logic, regional-value calculations and supplier declarations | Existing vendors already combine AI and origin workflows |
| [HMRC Trade Tariff developer portal](https://hub.dev.trade-tariff.service.gov.uk/) | Dated tariff-data integration resources | Transaction-level evaluation should link legal tariff versions rather than infer them from aggregate shares |

The plausible application is a reporting/audit component that distinguishes
changes in legal eligibility from claimed qualification improvement. Its buyer
could be a trade-policy evaluator, enterprise trade-compliance team, or a
provider assessing its own service outcomes. This is a product hypothesis, not
demonstrated demand.

An operational extension would need client declarations, tariff-line mappings,
effective dates, origin evidence, and actual duty amounts. It should log which
source change explains each reported saving. The current public-data package
demonstrates aggregate accounting only. It does not determine origin or certify
legal compliance.

Potential value includes avoiding incorrect credit for tariff changes, recognizing
benefits from ceasing unnecessary preference processes, and preserving audit
trails during law changes. Commercial validation would require a permissioned
customer dataset and a measured before/after audit with a credible comparison.
No patent search, legal novelty assessment, revenue forecast, or claimed patent
protection is included.
